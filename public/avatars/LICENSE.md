# 3D avatar assets

The human characters in `models/` (and the portraits in `thumbs/`) are derived from the
**Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library** — https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox —
including its ARKit-compatible facial blendshapes (contributed by Fang Ma and Matias Volonte).

They were converted from FBX to optimized glTF (merged vertices, a curated subset of
ARKit + viseme blendshapes, WebP textures, meshopt compression) for real-time use in the browser.
